Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Lain Social Feed ("Lain", "we", "our", or "the Service") is a Discord social-feed integration that allows authorized social media accounts to be monitored for newly published public content.

This Privacy Policy describes the information processed by Lain and how that information is used.

1. Information We Process

When a user connects a supported social media account, Lain may process information provided by that platform, including:

  • Account identifiers
  • Usernames and display names
  • Public profile information
  • Public content identifiers
  • Post or video URLs
  • Captions, titles, and other public content metadata
  • Publication timestamps
  • Thumbnails or cover images

2. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through connected platforms is used only to provide Lain's social-feed functionality.

This includes:

  • Identifying authorized accounts
  • Retrieving recent public content
  • Detecting newly published content
  • Preventing duplicate notifications
  • Generating notifications in configured Discord channels
  • Maintaining authorized social-feed connections

Lain does not sell personal information or use connected account information for advertising.

3. TikTok Data

When a user connects a TikTok account, Lain may use TikTok's official developer APIs after receiving authorization from the account owner.

Lain may request permissions including:

  • user.info.basic — used to identify the authorized TikTok account.
  • video.list — used to retrieve recent public videos belonging to the authorized TikTok account.

TikTok information is used to detect newly published public videos and generate Discord notifications requested by the user.

Lain does not use these permissions to modify, delete, or interact with the user's TikTok content.

4. Authentication Information

Some integrations require access tokens, refresh tokens, or similar authentication credentials.

These credentials are used only to authenticate requests to the relevant platform and maintain integrations authorized by the user. Authentication credentials are not intentionally displayed in public Discord messages or social-feed notifications.

5. Discord Notifications

Lain posts notifications to Discord channels configured by authorized Discord server administrators.

Notifications may contain publicly available information such as a creator name, caption, title, thumbnail, publication time, and link to the original content.

6. Data Retention

Lain may retain limited information necessary to operate the Service, including:

  • Connected account identifiers
  • Social-feed configuration
  • Previously detected content identifiers
  • Deduplication information
  • Authentication information where required

Content identifiers may be retained to prevent the same content from being announced multiple times.

7. Data Sharing

Lain does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to services necessary to operate the integration, including Discord, TikTok, Spotify, Meta/Instagram, Twitch, X, hosting infrastructure, and other platforms explicitly connected by the user.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authentication credentials and other information processed by Lain.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

9. User Control and Data Deletion

Users may disconnect supported social media integrations or remove their configured Social Feed.

Users may also request deletion of information associated with their authorized account. Upon a valid request, associated configuration, authentication information, and stored social-feed data will be removed where reasonably possible and subject to applicable legal requirements.

10. Third-Party Services

Lain interacts with third-party platforms. Those services process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Lain's functionality, integrations, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

Questions regarding privacy, connected accounts, or data deletion requests may be directed to the operator of Lain Social Feed using the contact information provided with the Service.
